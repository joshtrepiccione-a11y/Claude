04 · Voice & Tone

Personality: Steady, plainspoken, dependable — the neighbor who shows up early and finishes the job right. Speak with a veteran's quiet confidence; never boast.

Do

  • Lead with service, integrity, precision
  • Use direct, declarative sentences
  • Reference South Jersey by name
  • Cite proof: 500+ 5-star reviews, OC Platinum
  • Honor the customer's home as a mission

Don't

  • Use jingoism or salute-and-slogan clichés
  • Talk down to customers or jargon-stack
  • Over-promise; under-deliver
  • Use emojis or all-caps shouting in body
  • Mix military imagery with combat tone

06 · Imagery, Pattern & Iconography
Roof Chevron
Star Field
Shingle Grid

Photography: Real crew, real homes, real South Jersey skies. Shoot during golden hour; favor low angles that emphasize the ridgeline. Always include a worker — never empty roofs.

Iconography: Single-weight 2 px strokes, square caps, no fills. Restrict to Navy, Red, or Olive. Avoid generic stock icons and any imagery depicting weapons or combat.
